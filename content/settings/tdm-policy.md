## 2. Machine-Readable Reservation

In compliance with the machine-readability requirements set forth in Art. 4(3) EU 2019/790, this reservation is declared and signaled through multiple machine-readable mechanisms:
- **HTTP Response Headers**: `X-TDM-Reservation: 1` and `TDM-Reservation: 1` sent on all root and asset responses.
- **Robots.txt Directive**: Directives disallowing recognized AI crawler user-agents (e.g. `GPTBot`, `CCBot`, `ClaudeBot`, `Google-Extended`, `Bytespider`, `Diffbot`).
- **HTML Meta Tags**: `<meta name="tdm-reservation" content="1">` embedded in the document `<head>`.
- **W3C Rights Manifestation**: Machine-actionable JSON-LD declarations linked via `https://example.com/rights`.

## 3. Scope of Permitted vs. Prohibited Uses

### Permitted Uses
- **Human Reading & Viewing**: Traditional human reading, manual personal browsing, and academic research.
- **Standard Search Indexing**: Standard search engine crawlers (e.g., Googlebot, Bingbot) solely for surface search indexing and navigation.
- **Academic Citation**: Fair academic commentary and short quotations with unambiguous credit to the author.

### Strictly Prohibited Uses
- **Generative AI Model Training**: Direct ingestion into datasets used to train Large Language Models (LLMs), multimodal foundation models, or diffusion models.
- **Style Emulation & LoRA Weight Extraction**: Creation of Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) weights, embeddings, or algorithmic checkpoints trained to replicate the author's code style, architectural syntax, or prose cadence.
