## Notes on Physical Optics & Hardware

Software developers spend their careers manipulating abstract symbolic representations, but computing is fundamentally an artifact of physical matter: etched copper traces on woven fiberglass substrates, gold bond wires, silicon dies, and thermal dissipation radiators.

This documentary photo album explores computing hardware under natural daylight and manual optical glass:

## Camera & Optical Methodology

- **Focal Length Discipline**: Shot exclusively using fixed 35mm and 50mm focal lengths to eliminate zoom distortion and maintain human spatial perspective.
- **Physical Aperture Control**: Wide apertures (f/1.4 to f/2.0) isolate critical depth planes while allowing subtle lens aberrations to render authentic micro-textures.
- **Monochrome Tonal Grading**: Relying on Ansel Adams' Zone System to balance high-contrast metallic specular highlights with deep shadowed heat sinks.
