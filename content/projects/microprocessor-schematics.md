## Drafting Philosophy

1. **Information Density with Structural Clarity**: Every trace represents an unambiguous transmission path; non-functional decoration is discarded.
2. **Visual Hierarchy**: Critical data paths are weighted with bold contour lines, while control lines and clock distributions use muted secondary weights.
3. **Hardware Truth**: The schematic models real silicon constraints—pinout spacing, impedance matching, and propagation delays.
